Honoring Valor Through Sportsmanship

Conceived with the vision of uniting youth through the spirit of cricket and instilling deep respect for our nation's protectors, Operation Sindoor Cricket Cup 2.0 brings together the region's finest cricketers at the legendary Sector 16 Stadium, Chandigarh.

Featuring 24 teams (16 Boys & 8 Girls), the tournament is played in the thrilling, explosive T10 leather ball format with certified officials, official colored jerseys, and high-stakes knockout drama.

Organized by Sarv Sewa Sashktikarn Sangthan (Regd), this event stands as a beacon of unity — bringing together young athletes, families, and communities to celebrate the values of discipline, teamwork, and national pride.

  • Certified leather ball format with professional umpires
  • Official colored jersey kits for every registered team
  • Boys & Girls divisions on the same prestigious stage
  • Anti-drug awareness campaign integrated throughout the event

Tribute to Armed Forces

A heartfelt salute to the brave martyrs and soldiers of the Indian Armed Forces. This tournament is our way of honouring their sacrifice, courage, and unwavering dedication to the nation.

Rivers of India — Our Roots

Each team is named after a sacred river of India — symbols of life, culture, and civilization. Just as rivers unite communities, cricket unites us across regions, languages, and traditions.

Say No to Drugs — Play Sports

We believe sport is the strongest weapon against substance abuse. This tournament invites the youth to channel their energy onto the field — because a nation that plays together, stays stronger together.

Venue — Sector 16, Chandigarh

The tournament is hosted at the prestigious Sector 16 Stadium, Chandigarh — a world-class arena that gives every player the professional stage they deserve.

TOURNAMENT
REGULATIONS

Standard T10 rules apply. Action-packed, fast-paced matches designed for pure entertainment and skill.

3 Over Powerplay

Overs 1 to 3 with standard limited-overs fielding circle restrictions.

Bowler Quota

Max 2 overs per bowler. Minimum 5 bowlers must be utilized.

DRS & Super Over

1 Review/innings (retained if successful). Super Over for ties; DLS for rain.

1 Hour Reporting

Teams must report 1 hour prior. Toss 15m before match. Strict scratching policy.

Kit & Equipment

Official playing apparel (jersey & trousers) provided by committee. Teams bring personal gear.
